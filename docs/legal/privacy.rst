**************
Privacy policy
**************

This policy explains which personal data are processed when you visit the pyRadPlan
documentation or use the browser version of pyRadPlan, as required by Articles 13 and 14 of the
EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

*Last updated: 24 November 2026*

Summary
=======

- We do not run any servers of our own. Both sites are static pages served by hosting providers,
  which see your IP address like any web server.
- We do not use analytics, tracking or advertising cookies of our own, and we do not receive any
  personal data about visitors.
- The browser version computes everything on your computer. The CT, structures, plans and
  results you load or create are never uploaded.

Documentation (docs.pyradplan.org)
==================================

Hosting
-------

The documentation is hosted by Read the Docs, Inc., 818 SW 3rd Ave #221-5841, Portland, OR
97204-2405, USA ("Read the Docs"), on the Read the Docs Community platform, with Cloudflare, Inc.
as the content delivery network in front of it. When you open a page, your browser sends the
data that are technically necessary to deliver it: IP address, date and time, the requested page,
the referring page, browser type and language. According to Read the Docs, its server logs
containing IP addresses and user agents are deleted after 10 days. Read the Docs also records
page views of hosted documentation for its own statistics and honours the "Do Not Track"
setting of your browser.

Cloudflare may set the strictly necessary cookie ``__cf_bm``, which distinguishes browsers from
automated bots and expires after 30 minutes of inactivity.

Advertising
-----------

As a free Community project, the documentation displays advertisements served by EthicalAds, the
advertising network of Read the Docs. According to EthicalAds, ads are selected only by the
content of the page and the visitor's country, the IP address is used in real time for
geo-targeting and fraud prevention and is not stored beyond log files that are deleted after at
most 10 days, no tracking cookies are set and no third-party code from advertisers is run.

Formulas
--------

Pages that contain mathematical formulas load the MathJax library from the jsDelivr content
delivery network (cdn.jsdelivr.net), which receives your IP address and the requested file.

Browser version (app.pyradplan.org)
===================================

Hosting
-------

The browser version and its landing page at https://app.pyradplan.org (also reachable as
https://e0404.github.io/pyRadPlan/) are hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc.,
88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA ("GitHub"). When you visit the site, GitHub
logs and stores your IP address for security purposes, regardless of whether you are signed in to
GitHub, together with the usual request data (date and time, requested files, referring page).
GitHub is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

Python packages
---------------

After you have acknowledged the notice on your first visit, the app downloads the Python packages
it needs (numpy, scipy and others) from the jsDelivr content delivery network (cdn.jsdelivr.net).
jsDelivr receives your IP address, the names of the requested files and the address of the site as
referrer. Nothing is downloaded from jsDelivr before you have acknowledged the notice.

Your planning data
------------------

pyRadPlan runs in your browser tab (WebAssembly, Pyodide). The files you open, the plans you create
and all results stay in the memory of that tab and are lost when you close or reload it. They leave
your computer only when you save a file yourself. We never receive them.

Storage on your device
----------------------

The app does not set cookies. It stores only what is strictly necessary for it to work (§ 25 (2)
no. 2 of the German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act, TDDDG):

- one entry in the browser's local storage recording that you have acknowledged the notice, so it
  is not shown again on every visit; it is kept until you clear the site data of your browser;
- a service worker, which enables the Stop button on GitHub Pages, and a flag in the session
  storage of the tab that prevents repeated reloads; the flag is deleted when you close the tab.

Purposes and legal basis
========================

The data described above are processed to deliver these sites securely and reliably and to keep
them free of charge for an open-source research project. The legal basis is our legitimate interest
in providing the documentation and the browser version (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). Storing and reading
information on your device happens only where it is strictly necessary to provide the service you
requested (§ 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG).

Read the Docs, Cloudflare and GitHub are based in the USA, so your data may be processed there.
Transfers to the USA rely on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework where the recipient is certified
under it, and otherwise on the standard contractual clauses of the European Commission.

Links to other sites
====================

Links to other websites (for example the source code on GitHub or the websites of the DKFZ) are
plain links. The other site receives data only when you click a link, and its own privacy policy
applies.

Your rights
===========

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17),
restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing
based on legitimate interests (Art. 21). To exercise them, contact the controller above. As we do
not receive or store personal data about visitors ourselves, requests about the data processed
by the hosting providers can also be sent to them directly.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority
(Art. 77 GDPR), in particular in the EU member state of your residence, your place of work or
the place of the alleged infringement. The authority responsible for us is the State Commissioner
for Data Protection and Freedom of Information Baden-Württemberg (Landesbeauftragter für den
Datenschutz und die Informationsfreiheit Baden-Württemberg).

You are not obliged to provide any personal data; without the technically necessary request data,
however, the pages cannot be delivered. No automated decision-making or profiling takes place.

Changes
=======

We update this policy when the sites or their hosting change. The current version is always
available at https://docs.pyradplan.org/en/latest/legal/privacy.html.
